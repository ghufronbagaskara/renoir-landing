# Renoir — first Instagram Story promo

## Intent

Concept 3 "Spreadsheet → system", widened so it promotes the whole studio and the website:

1. 0–3.45s hook: a messy order spreadsheet and chat noise. "Still running the business on spreadsheets?"
2. 3.45–7.6s turn (music drop): the sheet cleans itself, condenses to three KPIs, then the real Order & Field Sales screens.
3. 7.6–12.4s four services with real founder work: Websites (MAXY AI), Systems (Field Projects), UI/UX & identity (Industrial Coatings), Deploy & care (status card, no tool names).
4. 12.4–15.4s The Joint assembles from Design / Build / Run, wordmark, site tagline.
5. 15.4–19s the Renoir Indonesian home page scrolling on a phone; the floating "Apa masalahnya?" button is tapped.
6. 19–22s the button grows into the CTA: "Tell us what's broken." + "Ceritakan masalahnya" + "Link di bio".
