# Renoir IG Story v2 — storyboard (approved 2026-10-11)

Why v2: v1 felt plain (one repeated layout, flat paper, empty lower quarter), motion was uniform fade-and-rise,
the hook started static, the KPI beat flashed by with numbers that meant nothing, and the opening spreadsheet
had no relation to the system shown later.

## Visual system

- Paper `#f5f7fb`, ink `#17191c`, one cobalt `#1d4ed8`. Chaos is shown with density, rotation, strikethrough
  and hatched error cells, not extra colours.
- Oversized GeneralSans (150–260 px), lines revealed through masks; some giant type deliberately cropped by the frame.
- Static grain overlay (seeded, ~7%), faint editorial grid, vertical edge labels (left/right edges are not covered
  by Instagram UI). Key content stays between y=260 and y=1520.
- Depth: background / midground / foreground move at different rates. The camera pushes into real UI.
- Transitions: The Joint's three planes as a wipe (turn), ink wipe (brand), circle wipe from the tapped button (CTA).
- Reading time: words × 0.4 s, minimum 1.5 s on screen.

## Data continuity

The spreadsheet uses the Order & Field Sales vocabulary: No. Pesanan, Barang (60x Heat Resistant Alumunium 600C,
15x Thinner Khusus, Floor/Protective Coating, Waterproofing), Gudang (Cirebon, Bekasi), Tahap (Gudang, Driver,
Admin Pengiriman, Keuangan, Pajak), PIC, Batas, Piutang. The four chat questions in the hook are answered,
in the same order, by the real screens in scene 3.
